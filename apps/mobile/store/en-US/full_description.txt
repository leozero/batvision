BatVision is the app for your own media server. Run the free, open-source BatVision server at home, point it at your movies and TV series, and watch them anywhere: streamed over the internet, or downloaded to your phone for when you're offline.

This app needs a BatVision server. It does not include or sell any content: you watch the files you own, from a server you or your family run.

WATCH
• Browse your movies and series with posters, summaries and ratings
• Resume where you stopped, on any of your devices
• Choose the audio track and subtitles; your choice follows you through the series
• Picture quality adapts to your connection, or pick it yourself (Max, Medium, Low)
• Next episode in one tap, picture-in-picture when you leave the app

DOWNLOAD
• Download movies and episodes to watch offline, in a lighter version made by your server for phones
• Keep the next episodes of a series on your phone automatically, and watched ones are deleted for you
• Choose where files go (phone or SD card), how much space they may use, and whether mobile data may be used, with a monthly cap
• Downloads continue in the background, even with the app closed, and resume after an interruption
• Offline, the app opens on what's downloaded; your progress goes back to the server when you're online again

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account with us, no ads, no tracking: the app only talks to your server
• Two-factor sign-in, and your device token is kept in the Android keystore

BatVision is open source (GPL): https://github.com/leozero/batvision
